## Trade-offs

> Multiple ad blockers can really slow down your browser! Use Arc's built-in protection against ads, pop-ups, and trackers.

> Let Arc do more for you with Max, so you can do less. … Max features require that we send data to our AI partners.

> These Spaces will start fresh in your brand new Profile — so you'll have to log back in to websites, re-save your passwords, add extensions, etc. You can always switch back to your previous Profile though if you forgot something!

> Currently, notes larger than 1MB cannot be shared or synced. They'll still be available on this computer.
